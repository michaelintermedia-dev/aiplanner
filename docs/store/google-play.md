## Store listing

### English

**Short description (80):**
Say it, and it's planned: tasks, events and notes with reminders, by voice.

**Full description:**

Gatazee is a planner you talk to.

Hold the mic and say what's on your mind, the way you'd tell a friend: "Dentist next Tuesday at 4, remind me an hour before", "buy oat milk", "idea for the garden". Gatazee understands it and turns it into a task, an event or a note, with the date, time, place and reminders filled in. You check it, tap Save, and you're done. Gata azi: Romanian for "all done for today".

WHAT IT DOES
• Speak or type, in your own words. Pauses and second thoughts are fine.
• Tasks, events and notes, all in one feed, with a calendar (day, week, month).
• Reminders that fit: once, before an event, every day, on chosen days. They stop by themselves when an event is over.
• Repeating tasks and events: daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly.
• Change anything later by voice: "move it to Friday", "make it an event".
• Photos and documents on any entry. The AI can read them for you (with your permission): a flyer becomes an event, a bill becomes a task.
• Search everything, including what you said and what's in your photos. Quick views: Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Open tasks.
• Share a link, text or photo from any app into Gatazee.
• A home-screen widget to start recording with one tap.
• 17 languages, including Hebrew and Arabic (right-to-left).
• Themes, colour skins and wallpapers, light and dark.

YOUR DATA
• Nothing the AI proposes is saved until you confirm it.
• No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.
• Download all your data or delete your account anytime, in Settings.

Gatazee also works in your browser at app.gatazee.com, with the same account.

### Calendar permission (READ/WRITE_CALENDAR)

Only when the user turns on Settings - Calendar - "Show in this phone's calendar":
the app creates its own local "Gatazee" calendar and writes the user's events
into it. It never reads other calendars and sends nothing from the device's
calendars anywhere - so for Data safety, calendar data is not collected.

### Exact alarm permission (USE_EXACT_ALARM)

Declaration: "Calendar or alarm clock app". Reason: Gatazee is a calendar and
planner; users set reminders for events and tasks at exact times, and a late
reminder (Android delays inexact alarms by up to an hour) defeats its core
function.

## Data safety

Is data collected or shared? **Yes, collected.** Encrypted in transit: **Yes**.
Users can request deletion: **Yes** (in the app, Settings → Account → Delete account,
or by email). "Shared" = sent to a third party for its own purposes: **no** - OpenAI,
Resend and the hosting provider are service providers acting for us, which Play does
not count as sharing.
