Say it. Gata azi.

Gatazee is a planner you talk to. Say or type what's on your mind, and it becomes a task, an event or a note, with the date, place and reminders filled in. Gata azi is Romanian for "all done for today".

Just talk

Hold the mic and say it the way you'd tell a friend. Pauses and second thoughts are fine.

Reminders that fit

Once, before an event, every day or on chosen days. They stop by themselves when an event is over.

Everything in one place

Tasks, events and notes in one feed, with search, quick views, tags, photos and a calendar.

17 languages

From English, Hebrew and Russian to Arabic, Hindi, Chinese and Japanese.
